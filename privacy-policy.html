Privacy Policy

How Mughal Education Consultancy handles the information you share through this website.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Mughal Education Consultancy ("we", "us", "our") is a Pakistan-based education consultancy and travel agency providing independent student recruitment and education guidance, plus travel assistance. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it and how we handle it.

1. Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry form on this website, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details (phone, WhatsApp number and email address);
  • Your country, city and, optionally, your date of birth;
  • Your study preferences, including preferred university, course, study level, intake and previous qualification;
  • Your approximate tuition budget, if you choose to provide it;
  • Any message or questions you include;
  • Travel enquiry details such as departure city, destination, travel dates, passenger numbers and trip type; and
  • If you contact us on behalf of a university, your university or organisation, your role or department, and the subject of your partnership enquiry.

We do not ask for passport copies, national identity documents, financial account details or payment card information through this website. Please do not submit these through our forms.

2. How your information is used

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry by phone, WhatsApp or email;
  • Provide guidance about universities, programmes and application steps;
  • Assist with travel planning enquiries; and
  • Keep a record of our communication so we can help you consistently.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Legal basis and consent

We process your information because you have asked us to respond to your enquiry and, where required, because you have given consent by ticking the consent checkbox on our forms. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

4. How forms are delivered

Depending on how this website is configured, enquiry form submissions are either:

  • sent to a secure third-party form processing service and then forwarded to our business email; or
  • prepared in your browser and passed to your own email application or WhatsApp so that you can review and send the message yourself.

In the second case, the website itself does not store your enquiry. Third-party services we use have their own privacy policies.

5. Sharing with universities and service providers

If you ask us to help with a university application, we may share relevant details with the university or its admissions office as part of that process. We may also use service providers such as email, hosting and form-processing providers who process information on our behalf.

We do not describe ourselves as an official representative of any university unless a written agreement is in place. Admission decisions are made by the universities.

Our Code of Conduct explains how we handle student information in practice, including what we will not do with it.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website uses essential storage to remember your cookie notice choice. If analytics is enabled by the site owner, analytics cookies may be used to understand how visitors use the site. You can decline non-essential cookies using the cookie notice, and you can clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

7. Data retention

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to support an application or travel booking. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, subject to any legal or record-keeping obligations.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share, including using HTTPS on this website. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive documents unless we have agreed a secure method with you.

9. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to:

  • Confirm what information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete information we no longer need; or
  • Stop contacting you.

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

10. Children

This website is intended for prospective students and their parents or guardians. If you are under 18, please make enquiries together with a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
