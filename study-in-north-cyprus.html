Study Destination

Study in North Cyprus

A practical guide for Pakistani students exploring higher education opportunities in North Cyprus — study levels, popular fields and the documents universities may ask for.

Why North Cyprus

An international study environment

North Cyprus is home to a group of universities that welcome students from many countries. The right choice depends on your programme, budget and plans.

International Student Environment

Universities in North Cyprus host students from a wide range of countries, creating a multicultural campus experience.

Undergraduate & Postgraduate Options

Students can explore foundation, bachelor's, master's and, at some institutions, doctoral programmes.

Different Academic Fields

Fields range from medicine, dentistry and pharmacy to engineering, business, law, architecture and tourism.

Campus Facilities

Many universities publish details of libraries, laboratories, sports facilities and student services on their websites.

Accommodation Options

On-campus and off-campus accommodation options vary by university and should be confirmed directly.

Compare Universities

Exploring more than one institution helps you compare programmes, facilities, location and published costs.

Available Study Levels

Find the level that fits your background

Programme availability and admission requirements depend on the university and the specific course.

Preparation

Foundation Programmes

Preparatory study designed to build academic or language skills before a degree. Availability varies by university.

Undergraduate

Bachelor's Degrees

Four-year degree programmes in a wide range of academic and professional fields, subject to university entry criteria.

Postgraduate

Master's Degrees

Programmes for graduates who want to specialise further. Entry requirements are set by each university.

Research

PhD Programmes

Doctoral study is offered by some universities in selected fields. Research areas and supervision should be confirmed directly.

Programme duration and structure can differ between universities and fields. Always check the current programme page before applying.

Admission Requirements

Documents universities may request

Requirements vary by university, programme and study level. The list below covers documents commonly requested — the university will confirm exactly what it needs.

  • Valid passport
  • Academic certificates
  • Academic transcripts
  • Passport-size photographs
  • English-language evidence where required
  • Additional documents requested by the university

Check recognition for your plans. If you intend to use your qualification in Pakistan or another country, verify recognition and equivalence requirements with the relevant authority before you commit.

Before you apply

  • Confirm the programme is offered for your intended intake.
  • Check the language of instruction and any English requirement.
  • Review the current tuition fee and scholarship information published by the university.
  • Ask about accommodation options and application deadlines.
