Frequently Asked Questions

Questions students and parents ask

Straightforward answers about studying in North Cyprus, applications, documents, scholarships and travel — including what we can and cannot do.

How can I study in North Cyprus?

Start by identifying the study level and subject you want. Then review the universities that offer your programme, check their entry requirements and prepare the documents they ask for. You can apply directly to a university or ask Mughal Education Consultants for independent guidance through the process. Admission decisions are made by the university.

Which universities are available in North Cyprus?

Universities students commonly ask about include Near East University, Eastern Mediterranean University, Cyprus International University, Girne American University, European University of Lefke, Final International University and Onbeş Kasım Kıbrıs University. Each has its own programme catalogue, entry requirements and fee structure, so always check the official website.

Can Pakistani students apply?

Yes. Students from Pakistan can apply to North Cyprus universities, and the institutions welcome international applicants. Each university sets its own entry criteria, including academic requirements and, where relevant, English-language evidence. Confirm the requirements for your chosen programme before applying.

What documents are required for admission?

Requirements vary, but students are commonly asked for a valid passport, academic certificates, academic transcripts, passport-size photographs and English-language evidence where required. Some programmes ask for additional documents. The university confirms the exact list for your application.

Can I apply for undergraduate programmes?

Yes. Bachelor's degree programmes are offered across a wide range of fields. Entry requirements usually relate to your previous qualification, such as Intermediate or FSc, and may include English-language evidence. The university assesses each application against its own criteria.

Can I apply for master's programmes?

Yes. Master's programmes are available in many fields for students who already hold a bachelor's degree. Requirements typically include your degree certificate and transcript, and may include English-language evidence or programme-specific documents. Confirm details with the university.

Are scholarships available?

Many North Cyprus universities publish scholarship and tuition-discount information for international students. Eligibility, amounts and conditions are set by each university and can change between intakes. We can help you find the official information, but we do not award scholarships or guarantee a discount.

How long does university admission take?

Timelines vary by university, programme and intake. Some applications are reviewed quickly, while others take longer, especially where additional documents are requested. Apply as early as possible and check the university's published deadlines for your intended intake.

Can Mughal Education Consultants help with travel planning?

Yes. Alongside education guidance, Mughal Education Consultants provides travel assistance including flight booking enquiries, itinerary planning, travel document guidance and pre-departure support. Airline schedules, fares and conditions are set by the airlines and travel providers.

Does Mughal Education Consultants guarantee admission?

No. Admission decisions are made solely by the universities. Mughal Education Consultants provides independent guidance and application support, but we cannot guarantee admission, a scholarship or any particular outcome. Be cautious of anyone who promises a guaranteed place.

Does Mughal Education Consultants guarantee a visa?

No. Visa, entry and residence decisions are made by the relevant authorities. We can help you understand the documents and steps involved, but we cannot influence or guarantee the decision. Always confirm current entry requirements before travelling.

Is Mughal Education Consultants an official agent of any university?

We are an independent education consultancy. We describe ourselves as an official agent or representative of a university only where a written agreement is in place. Our Code of Conduct sets out the standards we follow, and universities can review our partnership information before deciding whether to work with us.

What does Mughal Education Consultants charge?

Any service fee we charge is explained to you before you commit, including what it covers. University tuition and application fees are set by the university, not by us. This website does not process payments, and we never ask for payment in exchange for a guaranteed admission, scholarship or visa.

How are my documents and personal information handled?

We ask only for what an enquiry or application needs, and we share your details with a university only to support the application you have asked us to help with. We do not request passport copies or financial information through this website, and we do not sell your data. Never send original documents by post unless we have agreed a safe method with you.

How can I contact Mughal Education Consultants?

You can call or message us on WhatsApp, send an email, or use the enquiry form on this website. Contact details are shown in the header, footer and on the contact page. We aim to reply during normal business hours.

A note on accuracy: university programmes, fees, deadlines and entry requirements can change. Always confirm current information on the official university website before you apply or travel.
