About Near East University

Near East University is a large, comprehensive university based in Nicosia, North Cyprus. Its campus brings together academic buildings, health facilities and student services, and it is one of the institutions students ask us about most often.

Students considering Near East University should review the university's official programme catalogue for current entry requirements, language of instruction, tuition fees and scholarship information. Programme availability can change between intakes.

Study levels

Undergraduate and postgraduate programmes are offered in a range of fields, subject to the university's current catalogue. Foundation, English preparatory or doctoral options may also be available depending on the university and department.

Entry requirements vary by programme. The university assesses each application individually and confirms the documents it requires.

Admission documents

  • Valid passport
  • Academic certificates and transcripts
  • Passport-size photographs
  • English-language evidence where required
  • Any additional documents requested by the university

Fees and scholarships

Tuition fees, scholarship options and payment plans are set by the university. We can help you find the current official information and understand what to ask, but we do not set fees or award scholarships.

Information on this page is general and may change. Please confirm programme availability, entry requirements, deadlines and costs directly with Near East University before making a decision.
