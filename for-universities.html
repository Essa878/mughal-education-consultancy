Who We Are

A reachable point of contact in Pakistan

Mughal Education Consultants is an education consultancy and travel agency based in Dina, Pakistan. Alongside travel assistance, we guide students and families through the process of exploring universities in North Cyprus.

Our work starts with a conversation: what the student wants to study, what qualifications they hold, what they can realistically afford and when they hope to start. From there we explain the options and help prepare an application that is complete and accurate.

We are deliberately clear about our role. Admission, scholarship and visa decisions belong to the university and the relevant authorities. We do not describe ourselves as an official agent or representative of any university unless a written agreement is in place, and we do not use university logos without permission.

We are now looking to formalise relationships with universities in North Cyprus through written agent agreements. If you manage agent relations, we would welcome the chance to introduce ourselves properly.

Why work with us

  • A single, reachable contact in Pakistan for student enquiries and follow-up;
  • Applications prepared with complete, consistent and genuine information;
  • Students told plainly what the university decides and what we can support;
  • Pre-departure and travel assistance from the same team;
  • Marketing that avoids invented rankings, fees, statistics or outcomes.

Our code of conduct

What We Do

Support across the student journey

The practical work we carry out before, during and after an application.

Prospective student enquiries

We speak with students and families in Pakistan, explain the options and establish whether North Cyprus study is a realistic fit before an application is prepared.

Programme and entry information

We present programmes, entry requirements and published fees using the information the university supplies — not invented rankings, discounts or outcomes.

Application preparation

We help students assemble a complete application: correct personal details, qualification history, programme choice and the documents the university asks for.

Document checks

We check that files are legible, complete and consistent before submission, and we tell students what the university still needs. We never alter documents.

Communication and follow-up

We keep the student informed about requests from the university and help them respond within the deadlines the university sets.

Pre-departure support

Once an offer is accepted, we help with travel planning, itinerary questions and practical preparation for arrival in North Cyprus.

How We Work

From first enquiry to departure

A consistent process we are happy to adapt to your university's own agent instructions.

01

Student enquiry

A student contacts us by form, WhatsApp, phone or email and explains what they want to study.

02

Eligibility conversation

We review qualifications, study level, budget and timing, and explain which options appear suitable.

03

Application prepared

The student provides accurate details and genuine documents; we check the file against the university's checklist.

04

Submitted through the agreed route

The application is submitted using the process the university confirms — its portal, email or agent channel.

05

Offer and departure

The university decides. We explain the outcome, help with next steps and support pre-departure planning.

Our Commitments

Standards we will hold to

These are the promises we make to students, and the same standards we would expect to agree with a partner university.

Accurate marketing

We describe programmes, fees and requirements using current university information. We do not publish invented rankings, scholarship percentages or admission statistics.

No guarantees

We never promise admission, a scholarship, a visa or a particular outcome. Those decisions belong to the university and the relevant authorities.

Genuine documents only

We do not create, edit or submit falsified transcripts, certificates, bank statements or identity documents, and we stop working with anyone who asks us to.

Transparent costs

Any service fee we charge is explained to the student before they commit. University fees are set by the university and are never presented as our own.

Student data

Student details are shared with a university only to support the application the student has asked us to help with. We do not sell data or send bulk marketing lists.

Clear identity

We identify ourselves as an independent consultancy. We do not use a university's name or logo to imply official status without written permission.

Working Together

What helps a partnership work

We are set up to follow a university's own process rather than asking for exceptions. In practice, the things that make an agent relationship work are:

  • Current programme, entry requirement and fee information we can quote accurately;
  • A clear application route — portal, email or agent channel — and the documents you require;
  • A named contact for agent questions and application follow-up;
  • A written agreement covering representation, commission and termination;
  • Approved marketing materials and guidance on what we may publish;
  • A simple reporting routine, such as a monthly summary of enquiries and applications.

What we will not do

  • Claim official representation without a signed agreement;
  • Publish university logos without written permission;
  • Invent fees, rankings, scholarships or admission statistics;
  • Submit altered or falsified documents;
  • Promise a visa, admission or scholarship outcome;
  • Share student details beyond what an application requires.
