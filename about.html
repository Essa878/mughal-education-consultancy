About Mughal Education Consultancy

Our Story

Guidance grounded in clarity and care

Mughal Education Consultancy helps students and families make sense of studying abroad — one conversation at a time.

Mughal Education Consultancy is a Pakistan-based education consultancy and travel agency providing independent international education guidance and travel assistance.

We help students interested in studying abroad explore university options, understand admission requirements and prepare for their educational journey.

Our focus is on North Cyprus, where students can explore a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate study opportunities.

Alongside education guidance, Mughal Education Consultancy offers travel planning assistance to help customers prepare for international journeys.

Our mission is to provide clear information, personalised support and professional customer service throughout the process.

Independent Guidance

Clear about what we can — and cannot — do

Trust starts with an honest description of our role in your journey.

Universities decide

Admission, scholarship and visa decisions are made by the relevant universities and authorities. We help you prepare, but we cannot guarantee an outcome.

Official information first

Programme details, fees and deadlines can change. We encourage students to confirm current information on each university's official website.

No hidden claims

We do not describe ourselves as an official university representative unless a written agreement is in place.

See how the process works

Working With Universities

University partnerships

We are an independent consultancy, and we are open about it. Where a university chooses to work with us formally, we want that relationship set out in a written agent agreement covering representation, commission, marketing approval and how the agreement ends.

Until such an agreement exists, we describe ourselves only as an independent education consultancy — never as an official agent or representative. Universities and their agent-relations teams can review our services, standards and the way we handle student information before deciding whether to work with us.

For universities Code of conduct

What we can provide

  • A named contact for agent questions and follow-up;
  • Applications prepared with complete, genuine documents;
  • Clear explanations to students about who decides what;
  • Marketing that avoids invented rankings or outcomes;
  • Business details on request for due diligence;
  • A written code of conduct we can be held to.
